About Me

I am a game developer who thrives on complex problems and brings my own creativity to solving them. I have years of professional experience in C# and Unity, including shipping multiple mobile games to full public release, where I've built everything from gameplay systems and menus to live-service features. I also have a background of working with C++ and Unreal Engine 5.

I'm quick to learn new tools, systems, and languages, and I'm comfortable taking ownership of features that reach deep into a project. I focus on the programming and technical side, but I can step into game design, UI/UX design, and level design when needed. I do my best work alongside talented people, building products that are fun, interesting, and useful.
