Nathan Wildofsky

Game Developer and Designer

I am a game developer that enjoys the challenge of solving complex problems while also adding my own personal spin and creativity. I have years of experience in the C# and C++ languages, I am well versed in using Unity, and I am familiar with Unreal Engine 5. That being said, I am quick to learn new tools and programs and eager to sink my teeth into new languages. I like to focus on the programming and technical aspects of projects but I have the experience to put on the game design, level design, and web design hats when needed. I enjoy working with a team full of talented people and creating products that are fun, interesting, and useful.
